> `nekorisya.print_projects()`

Most of what happens here eventually finds its way into a repository.

We maintain and experiment with projects across different areas of software development. Every repository has its own scope, documentation, and contribution guidelines, so check the individual project before contributing.

If you find something useful, interesting, broken, or simply weird, you're welcome to take a look.

---

> `nekorisya.contribute()`

Interested in contributing?

Read the repository's `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` first. If a project doesn't have its own contribution guidelines, follow the community-wide guidelines.

Whether you're fixing a bug, improving documentation, proposing an idea, or building something new, contributions are welcome.
